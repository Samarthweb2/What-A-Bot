# Proxy Study Materials

## Overview
As per Section B of the plan, this is a proxy study involving 3 participants (friends/classmates, explicitly labelled as proxies, not merchants) running for 30 minutes.

### Goal
Evaluate the time and correctness of fulfilling a budget-constrained order manually vs. using the WhatsApp agent.

### Setup
- Participants will do **one task manually** (using a catalog sheet).
- Participants will do **one matched task via the agent**.
- The order is alternated between participants to reduce recall bias.
- Quantities are varied between tasks.

---

## Task Sheet

### Participant 1
- **Order**: Manual first, then Agent
- **Task A (Manual)**: You have a budget of ₹550. Find a combination of 10 A5 ruled notebooks from the provided catalog sheet. Mixed brands are allowed. Provide the final list and total cost.
- **Task B (Agent)**: You have a budget of ₹600. Ask the WhatsApp agent for a combination of 12 A5 ruled notebooks. Mixed brands are allowed. Complete the order.

### Participant 2
- **Order**: Agent first, then Manual
- **Task A (Agent)**: You have a budget of ₹450. Ask the WhatsApp agent for a combination of 8 A5 ruled notebooks. Mixed brands are allowed. Complete the order.
- **Task B (Manual)**: You have a budget of ₹500. Find a combination of 9 A5 ruled notebooks from the provided catalog sheet. Mixed brands are allowed. Provide the final list and total cost.

### Participant 3
- **Order**: Manual first, then Agent
- **Task A (Manual)**: You have a budget of ₹700. Find a combination of 14 A5 ruled notebooks from the provided catalog sheet. Mixed brands are allowed. Provide the final list and total cost.
- **Task B (Agent)**: You have a budget of ₹650. Ask the WhatsApp agent for a combination of 13 A5 ruled notebooks. Mixed brands are allowed. Complete the order.
